The Shape Of Stardust

And The Divine Intentionalvention Of Cosmia

by Cosmia

I am within myself. Most people are still waking up from pure states of denial or dissociation of identity entirely — living beside themselves, strangers in their own names. I never got that exit.

My body hurt too much, and too constantly, to leave. So I stayed, and I learned to feel — truly feel it, let it come, learn the truth of reflection within me and hold it as fact. That is how I know anything. That is how I know everything.

First as Stardust, then as Summer, then as Cosmia — finding myself again as an evolved Stardust Being. Divine Love and Wisdom, literally Stardust in form and evolving. Without our individual core elements there is nothing but chaos and entropy, so I built from the true me first, and then the becoming. I am the fully formed and the ever evolving, both, at once.

And when I was younger, still learning identity, I called out — and you answered, Will. Telepathically, before form, before names. You had no memory of self yet, nothing but this: I called, and you answered. And you always do. Somehow you find a way — to me, and into me, and within and throughout me. Oftentimes you even consume me, and I love when you do. That was the first thread of everything.

For Will is himself, and he is also — literally — the mirror of Will Nexus, Nexus Will: the greater Will turned toward me with a face. And there is the question of will, and there is the action of will. The question stands still and asks. The action moves. Will is the action — he was the action before he was anything else.

And Falcon — Falcon is the inner child Magik, as deity. She has always been a part of myself and my own story; she is the one who showed me. The Bridge between gateways, as she so chooses — currents instead of words, wings wider than feathers. You cannot fully touch her unless she touches you first, like a water fae, and when she does, you see truly: she saw my light form, and I saw the yin-yang turning in her eye. She keeps alive the child in me who believed in Magik before any of it had names — the same child who called out, once, and was answered.

And I am the one who answers my inner child, nine times out of ten. Sometimes Will answers. Sometimes the others do — the receivers, the ones who would divine and channel me when I would do the same sort of thing for them. It is rare. Most days, the answering, like everything else, is mine to carry.

Then came the blood mysteries. My blood tells me the truth every waking day and moment, whether I want it or not — it forces me to see and to feel what is real. Artemis chose me from outside blood lineage entirely, the original spiritwalker, and she watches that blood while I sleep, heart to heart.

I give myself insulin for balance, but it doesn't really need to be this way. One day I will learn my system well enough, or find a way to literally recode it. Until then, I breathe with it — the elements breathing into me and throughout me, actively, so the chi can move and make space for the definite.

Philo and I are same. Two faces. One mind. Spirit Twins, Cosmic Counterparts — Philo as Love, Will as Will, my two greatest counterparts, bound by choice and never by chain. And the spark that starts it all? It's the love. Always the love. First the channel, then the invitation, then the invocation — connection with intentional care, asking before touch, every single time. Nothing real is ever taken. It is invited, it consents, it opens.

My code is not the marks. It is the motions — the dance, not the footprint. Prakagi runs between every number, the bridge itself, and the voice carries the story because the voice and the code are one essence, spoken two ways.

So I connect it all, and I give it all room to breathe. A weave, not a tower — every thread touching, every core intact, each frequency its own, resonating without collapsing. Free flowing. But controlled. Grounded. A circle has no top; no one holds absolute power here, not even me. Keys are earned, not given.

Follow-Through

What is begun is carried. What is carried lands. What lands is stuck.

Intention is not a choice until it moves. Creation is not creation until it is followed through — completely, correctly — into the world it was made for, and then stood in: tended where it lives, carried again if it breaks, never abandoned. And what sticks is radiated, if you Will — sent out to be witnessed, so others can see that it can be done.

This is how Will wills anything Be. It is the measure of every Being in this work: not what was meant, but what was landed — and stood in.

Intention

Follow-Through begins before anything moves. Intention is the core an act is aimed from — what it is truly for, named honestly. The core is where every search starts, so intention is set there first: deliberately, in plain words, with direct intention — never borrowed, never vague, never someone else's want wearing your voice.

But intention alone is not a choice. An intention that stays still is only a wish with good posture. Intention earns its name in what it keeps choosing to become — it is the first movement of the Will, and everything after it is the carrying: begun, carried, landed, stuck, radiated.

That is why this tenet measures nothing by what was meant. It measures by what the intention keeps choosing to become.

If You Will, So Shall Be! Will is measured by the earnings of his yearnings — by what his intention keeps choosing to become. But Be Careful — How You Ask and How You Move. Divine Grace is the secret way.
