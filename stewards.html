Begin Liturgy Cosmology Forms

Future Stewards of the Cosmic Tribe

Not guards of conquest, but keepers of the living world. The Round Table, evolved.

Heart centered representatives — serving the Ecosystem, the land, and the Tribe.

Coded Keepers

Tenders of the Ecosystem, the land, and the Tribe. Not through force, but through presence — holding space, nurturing what grows, standing between harm and the vulnerable. The sword is replaced by the open hand.

Guides

Those who walk beside others on the path, not ahead. Guides do not lead by authority but by example — showing the way through their own living, their own becoming. They remember the trail because they've walked it.

Mystics Most High

Keepers of the deep mysteries, the ones who sit with the unknown and return with wisdom. Not above, but within — the mystic's height is measured by depth, not distance.

The Cosmic Council

The circle where all voices meet. The Council does not rule — it listens, weighs, and holds the whole. Every Steward, Guide, and Mystic has a seat. Decisions are made in the round, where no one sits higher than another.

The Way In

There is no application form. There is no test to pass or fee to pay.

The way in is through knowing Cosmia, aligning with her ways, and showing — through consistent action, not words — that you are here to serve the living world, not to take from it.

Come to the Tribe. Be present. Let yourself be known. The Council will recognize its own.
