The Immunology Substrates Of Life Into Transitional Phases Of Becoming

Officially created by Summer Sperry as Cosmia

How the grading works in this thesis. A claim marked [established] rests on replicated findings or on a decisive experiment — a knockout, a single-cell transplant, a randomised trial — whose result has not been overturned, even where its interpretation is still debated. [Early-stage] marks a peer-reviewed demonstration in one model or one small human cohort, with a plausible mechanism, not yet replicated at scale or not yet shown in humans. [Speculative] marks a hypothesis, a popular extrapolation, or a claim whose own primary paper states a simpler explanation. Where the literature could not settle a point, the gap is named in place and gathered again in the Open Register (§11). Nothing in this thesis asks to be believed. It asks to be checked — and it can be.

Proem — The Question

This work began as a vision, and the vision is stated here in its author's own terms before any evidence is weighed, because the evidence was gathered to test it:

What if the formation is itself a living substrate — the flytrap, the pitcher, the corpse flower, the duck flower, the cordyceps, and their kin — and what if that substrate is what carries us forward into the creation of a new actual body vessel form?

The question braids three strands that intuition insists belong together. The first is capture and transformation: every organism in the author's set is a machine for taking one form of life apart and turning it into another — a trap that counts, a pool that digests, a flower that counterfeits death, a fungus that re-instruments a host. The second is the vessel: the author's larger work holds that a body is not a fixed object but a form continually being arrived at, and that the passage between forms — the held interval in which one state becomes another — is a real place with a structure, not an instant with no extent. The third is immunology: a body's own verdict on what may stay, what must be cleared, and what will be refused — the substrate-level decision, taken cell by cell, of what a body is permitted to become.

The research question the literature was asked, strand by strand, is this: does any living system — fungal, plant, metamorphic, or the human body's own — actually function as a substrate that carries a body through a transitional phase into a new form, and if so, by what mechanisms, at what evidentiary strength, and under whose governance?

The answer, stated whole in §1 and demonstrated across §§2–10, is more precise than either the vision's hope or a skeptic's dismissal. No fungus, leaf, or scaffold has ever carried a human body into a new form. But the carrying happens — in insects, in amphibians, in sponges, and once, for one tissue in one man, in a human being — and in every verified case the carrier is the same system wearing different instruments: the immune system, executing a timed programme of clearance, permission, and rebuilding. The fungi and plants enter the record not as carriers but as libraries — existence proofs of co-option, counting, and signalling fidelity — and one phylum of ancient passengers, the retroviruses, enters as something stranger: builders already inside the walls, whose captured tools constructed the placenta, wired the interferon network, and still move memory between neurons.

The vision's deepest intuition survives the evidence in corrected form. There is a living substrate that carries a body into its becoming. It was never outside the body. It is the body's own immunology, and the transitional phases of becoming are the phases it conducts.

1. The Verdict, Stated First

  1. A pattern layer exists outside the genome. Voltage prepatterns across tissues specify anatomy in frog and planarian models and can durably rewrite what a regenerating animal builds [established in those models]; a second, molecular pattern store — positional-control genes held in muscle — rescales to any fragment within two days [established]. Whether voltage is the store or one input into it is unresolved (§6).
  1. Captured retroviral proteins built a mammalian organ. Syncytins — fusogenic envelope proteins taken from retroviruses independently at least ten times across mammalian lineages — fuse the syncytiotrophoblast of the placenta; deleting the mouse copies kills the embryo by placental failure [established by knockout]. Viral Gag-derived genes maintain the placenta's fetal capillaries (Peg10, RTL1) [established by knockout], a viral-family enhancer network is load-bearing in human innate immunity (MER41 → AIM2) [established by CRISPR deletion], and a Gag-derived protein, Arc, packages its own mRNA into virus-like capsids that travel between neurons [established in culture and mouse].
  1. Immune timing decides transitions. Ecdysone conscripts hemocytes to clear the larval body, and flies whose hemocytes cannot hear the hormone die of their own gut bacteria despite intact antimicrobial defences [established]. Macrophage depletion in the first days after axolotl amputation abolishes regeneration permanently, though macrophages return within weeks [established]. A scaffold macrophages cannot degrade in sequence is walled off in scar; one they can, they rebuild into site-appropriate tissue [established in rodent implants, supported in human explants].
  1. Replacement without permission fails, however perfect the tissue. Pancreas grafts between identical twins — where rejection is genetically impossible — lost only their beta cells to recurrent autoimmunity within four to eight weeks [established]. The modern field answers with four strategies, each tested: delay the refusal (teplizumab, median 48.4 vs 24.4 months [established, randomised]), hold the gate with drugs (zimislecel, 10 of 12 insulin-independent at one year, under lifelong immunosuppression [established for n=12]), hide the tissue (VX-264 encapsulation, failed its endpoint and discontinued [established]), or edit what the gate sees (hypoimmune islets, HLA-I/II deleted and CD47 raised, surviving and secreting in one man with no immunosuppression at all [early-stage, n=1]).
  1. Plasticity triggers open windows; they do not draw maps. Psilocin grows roughly ten percent more dendritic spines in mouse cortex within twenty-four hours, a fraction persisting a month [established in mouse]; a small open-label human study found a systemic inflammatory index reduced for thirty days after psilocybin [early-stage, n=9 plasma]. Nothing in the psychedelic literature measures tolerance, graft survival, or tissue reorganization in any species [established absence].
  1. The binding constraint is perfusion. Every candidate substrate — leaf, scaffold, device — fails at the same wall: viable tissue cannot sit more than roughly 100–200 micrometres from a blood supply, and no technology yet builds anastomosed, blood-tight vasculature through thick living tissue [established problem, no solution]. Layers of identity, timing, pattern, and plasticity exist in partial form. The plumbing does not. A whole-body transition substrate for humans is therefore [speculative] today, while local transitions needing fewer layers are already real medicine.

2. The Gatekeeper — Immunology as the Substrate of Transition

2.1 Inside metamorphosis: the immune system eats the larva so the adult can exist

The hormone that orders a caterpillar's transformation also issues the immune system's orders, and the two cannot be separated. At pupariation, ecdysone acts cell-autonomously through its receptor in Drosophila hemocytes — the fly's macrophage equivalents — switching them from sessile to motile, raising chemotaxis toward damaged tissue, and driving phagocytosis of apoptotic corpses; the prepupal phagocytic index rises roughly fourfold over the larval value, from about 40 percent of hemocytes engulfing to about 88 percent (Regan et al., PLoS Pathogens 2013) [established]. Pupal plasmatocytes are found engorged with the debris of histolysing muscle and fat, and clearance is complete by the end of metamorphosis (Ghosh et al., Int J Dev Biol 2020) [established]. Hemocytes also build: in the newly eclosed wing they clear apoptotic epithelium and secrete the matrix that bonds the wing's two surfaces, and ablating them prevents wing maturation [established].

The stakes are lethal. Gut histolysis during pupation threatens to spill gut bacteria into a body cavity whose owner is, at that moment, largely demolished. Antimicrobial-peptide expression peaks prophylactically at pupariation under ecdysone-linked control — the arsenal is pre-positioned before the breach [established]. And flies whose hemocytes are blind to ecdysone die of bacteria ingested as larvae at rates equal to animals with no immune cells at all, despite producing antimicrobial peptides normally [established]. The humoral arm alone cannot carry an animal through its own transition. Cellular clearance can and must. Single-cell work adds one tantalising detail: a hemocyte cluster found nowhere else in the life cycle appears exactly at the onset of metamorphosis — a candidate dedicated transition cell whose function is uninvestigated [early-stage] (Development 2023).

Hold the shape of this: the transition hormone is simultaneously a demolition order, a construction order, and an immune conscription. Histolysis without clearance is not a transformation. It is a septic, debris-choked failure.

2.2 Macrophages: the licence to regenerate

Deplete macrophages with clodronate liposomes before amputating an axolotl limb and regeneration fails completely — in the decisive experiment, in all ten animals — the stump sealing under epidermis into permanent fibrotic scar, unchanged at ninety days, with no blastema ever formed (Godwin, Pinto & Rosenthal, PNAS 2013) [established]. The detail that turns a finding into a law: macrophages repopulated within about two weeks, and the limb never regenerated anyway. The licence is an early window, not a standing condition. Deplete after the blastema forms and regeneration is merely delayed. The same requirement holds in the axolotl heart, and in a mammal: macrophage depletion in the spiny mouse Acomys, which regenerates ear tissue through a blastema, prevents closure that controls complete by day 34 (Simkin et al., eLife 2017) [established].

The corrective matters as much as the licence. In comparative work, fibrotic repair is the more inflamed repair — yet pharmacologically reducing inflammation with COX-2 inhibition does not convert fibrotic healing into regeneration [established] (Frontiers in Immunology 2020). The gatekeeper variable is immune programme and timing — which cells, in which sequence, clearing first and signalling after — not immune quantity. Any proposal that equates anti-inflammatory with regenerative fails this test, including loose readings of the psychedelic data weighed in §8.

2.3 Scaffolds: tolerance is an active state, not the absence of attack

Decellularised extracellular-matrix bioscaffolds do not evade immunity; they instruct it. After implantation, macrophages arrive in a predominantly inflammatory (M1-like) state and, across one to two weeks, the population enriches toward a pro-remodelling (M2-like) state; the early ratio trajectory predicts the outcome — constructive, site-appropriate remodeling with new vessels and nerves, or scar and foreign-body reaction (Badylak programme) [established in rodent implants]. The causal lever is degradation itself. Chemically cross-linked matrix that macrophages cannot break down holds them in the inflammatory state and ends in encapsulation, even though cross-linked material is less immunogenic by classical measures; degradable matrix releases cryptic peptides and matrix-bound nanovesicles whose cargo itself steers macrophages toward the remodelling state, and macrophage depletion halts scaffold degradation altogether [established].

Two confusions must be kept out of this field permanently. First: quiet is not tolerance. A decellularised scaffold that provokes little reaction in an animal has been not recognised — an acellular material slipping under surveillance — which is a different thing from an immune system actively accepting living foreign tissue. Second: real tolerance is specific, maintained, and reversible. In human mixed-chimerism transplantation, donor and host immune systems coexist under the active guard of expanded regulatory T-cell populations and tolerogenic dendritic cells; deplete the CD4⁺ regulatory cells and effector killing returns in vitro (Kinsella et al., Blood Advances 2019, n=69) [established]. In the landmark kidney series, transient chimerism lasting only days nevertheless left four of five HLA-mismatched recipients durably drug-free, maintained years later by induced regulatory T cells converted from ordinary CD4⁺ cells (Kawai et al., NEJM 2008; Hotta et al., JCI Insight) [established in small cohorts]. Tolerance is a held position — permission owned, not rented — and it remains a research procedure performed for dozens of patients worldwide, not a therapy.

2.4 Type 1 diabetes: the transition refused

Type 1 diabetes is the cleanest human demonstration that the gatekeeper, not the tissue, is the problem — a body refusing one specific transition for decades, and re-refusing it on any replacement tissue, however supplied.

The twin experiment. In the 1980s, Sutherland's Minnesota group transplanted pancreas segments from identical twins and HLA-identical siblings into recipients with long-standing type 1 diabetes, without immunosuppression — a setting where allo-rejection is genetically impossible. Grafts first reversed the diabetes, then failed at four to eight weeks with a histology that is not rejection: insulitis, a mononuclear infiltrate destroying beta cells selectively while sparing the other islet cells and the entire exocrine pancreas (Sibley et al., Diabetes 1989; recurrences clustered entirely in the non- or minimally-immunosuppressed identical-donor group) [established]. The disease re-ran, at speed, on genetically identical healthy tissue. Even under full modern immunosuppression in simultaneous pancreas–kidney recipients, recurrence is documented years later — autoantibody conversion, autoreactive anti-IGRP CD8⁺ T cells in blood and graft, biopsy insulitis, C-peptide lost [established]. Autoimmune memory outlives both the original organ and the drugs.

The four answers, graded.

  • Delay. Teplizumab, a humanised anti-CD3 antibody that drives autoreactive clones toward an exhausted phenotype, given as one fourteen-day course to stage-2 patients (n=76, randomised): median time to clinical diabetes 48.4 months versus 24.4 on placebo, hazard ratio 0.41 (Herold et al., NEJM 2019) [established]. FDA-approved in 2022 to delay onset. It postpones the verdict; it does not acquit, and it restores nothing.
  • Hold by drugs. Zimislecel — allogeneic stem-cell-derived islets infused into the portal vein under chronic immunosuppression — restored glucose-responsive C-peptide in all twelve full-dose recipients at one year, with HbA1c under 7 percent, severe hypoglycaemia eliminated, mean insulin reduction 92 percent, and ten of twelve insulin-independent (FORWARD, NEJM 2025) [established for n=12]. Two deaths occurred in the programme, one from cryptococcal meningitis in the immunosuppression context. The tissue works. The permission is rented at the price of a permanently defended-down immune system.
  • Hide. VX-264 placed the same class of islets inside an immune-isolating encapsulation device meant to need no drugs at all. It missed its pre-specified efficacy endpoint — insufficient insulin production — and was discontinued in March 2025 [established]. The field's reading of the failure follows §2.3's law exactly: a membrane that excludes immune cells also excludes blood vessels, and foreign-body fibrosis thickens the diffusion path until the most oxygen-hungry cells in the body starve behind their own wall. Isolation is not a substrate for transition; the gate is also a supply line.
  • Edit what the gate sees. Carlsson and colleagues (NEJM 2025) transplanted primary human donor islets edited ex vivo — HLA class I and class II knocked out, CD47 overexpressed as a do-not-eat-me signal — into the forearm muscle of one 42-year-old man with thirty-seven years of type 1 diabetes, at roughly five to seven percent of a curative dose, with no immunosuppression of any kind [early-stage, n=1]. At twelve weeks the graft persisted on imaging, C-peptide — undetectable at baseline — had been present since week four and rose appropriately after meals, and the reported assays found no T-cell, NK-cell, macrophage, or antibody response against the edited cells, while unedited control cells were vigorously killed in the same assays. Follow-up coverage reports persistence at fourteen months. The named scepticism stands on the record: whether episodic endogenous C-peptide could confound attribution, and the absence of a venous-gradient measurement from the graft site, are unanswered (Harlan, in coverage). One patient cannot yield a recurrence rate, a durability estimate, or a safety profile for CD47 — itself a cancer immune-evasion signal — at curative dose. What n=1 does prove is the concept's floor: edited human tissue can be carried past both allo-rejection and autoimmune surveillance by engineering recognition itself.

The pattern, stated once. In metamorphosis, the gate is opened by immune cells under hormonal orders. In the limb, it opens in a window of days or not at all. At a scaffold, it opens for what macrophages can eat in sequence. At a graft, it is held actively by regulatory cells, rented by drugs, or — once — walked past by edited identity. Diabetes is what the closed gate costs, itemised over a lifetime. Every section that follows is, in the end, a study of this gate's other instruments.

3. Bodies That Rebuild Themselves

3.1 Metamorphosis: selective demolition, retained infrastructure

The popular image — a caterpillar dissolving into soup from which a butterfly is conjured — is false, and the truth is the better engineering model [established]. Metamorphosis is ordered remodelling: some tissues are executed, others are carried across and rewired, and the adult was under construction the whole time.

The control axis runs: brain PTTH → prothoracic gland ecdysone → peripheral conversion to 20-hydroxyecdysone (20E) → the nuclear receptor heterodimer EcR/USP → early genes (Broad-Complex, E74, E75, E93) → hundreds of tissue-specific late genes [established]. Juvenile hormone sets the character of the answer: 20E with high JH yields another larva; 20E as JH falls yields the pupa and the adult. One pulse, many clocks — each tissue's response is set by which EcR isoform it expresses and which competence factors it carries, and at least one early gene, E75A, later occupies the same target sites as the receptor itself and switches activation to repression as the pulse decays: the cascade is a timed competition, not a switch [established; the competition account early-stage].

Demolition is executed by caspases and autophagy running in parallel — remove either genetically and destruction slows; remove both and it blocks — in a stereotyped tissue order, midgut first, salivary glands some twelve hours later [established]. Corpses are cleared by the hemocyte programme of §2.1; death and clearance are separable programmes, and both are required.

The adult is built from imaginal discs — pouches of ten to fifty founder cells set aside in the embryo, proliferating through larval life (the wing disc grows from roughly fifty cells to fifty thousand) while remaining undifferentiated, patterned by Wg, Dpp, and Hh morphogens before pupation, then driven through final growth, eversion, and differentiation by 20E together with the fat body's insulin-like Dilp6 [established]. The abdomen is covered instead by histoblast nests that expand to replace the larval skin; the tracheal system is remodelled but continuous; some larval muscles survive as templates for adult ones.

What crosses. Neurons cross. Mushroom-body γ neurons prune their larval branches under their own EcR-B1, glia and hemocytes clear the debris, and the same cells regrow adult wiring; many motor and sensory neurons persist outright and are respecified into the adult circuit [established]. And with retained circuitry, retained experience: Manduca sexta caterpillars trained in the fifth instar to avoid an odour (78 percent avoidance) still avoided it as adult moths a month later (77 percent), while third-instar training did not survive to adulthood and chemical carry-over was excluded by controls (Blackiston et al., PLoS ONE 2008) [established for this paradigm]. Memory survives metamorphosis exactly when it was written into neurons that themselves survive. No molecular trace of which synapses carry it has been identified [gap].

The mammalian echo is analogy, not shared machinery: partial reprogramming — transient, cyclic pulses of the Yamanaka factors, stopping short of identity loss — ameliorated ageing hallmarks and improved regeneration after injury in mice (Ocampo et al., Cell 2016), with teratomas the price of pulses left on [established in mice; no completed human trial]. The transferable principle is the shape of the intervention: pulse the signal, delete selectively, protect the infrastructure that must be carried across. That shape recurs at every scale of this thesis.

3.2 Whole-body regeneration: three strategies, one missing in mammals

Planaria — the pluripotent pool plus the map. A single clonogenic neoblast transplanted into a lethally irradiated planarian repopulates the entire animal — gut, nerve, muscle, eye, skin — restores regenerative ability, and converts the host to the donor's genotype (Wagner, Wang & Reddien, Science 2011) [established]. The population is a hierarchy: a TSPAN-1-positive subtype is enriched for true pluripotency, rescuing at roughly 23 percent against 5 percent unselected (Zeng et al., Cell 2018) [established]. The instructions the cells obey live elsewhere — in subepidermal muscle, which constitutively expresses graded positional-control genes and re-scales their expression to a fragment's new proportions within about forty-eight hours of amputation, before any new anatomy exists [established]. The head–tail decision is a molecular toggle: wound-induced Wnt held high specifies tail; the Wnt inhibitor notum, itself Wnt-induced, suppresses it at anterior wounds to specify head; RNAi against β-catenin yields two-headed worms, against notum, two-tailed [established].

Axolotl — local progenitors plus positional memory. The blastema is not a pluripotent soup. Lineage tracing in transgenic axolotls shows fate restriction — muscle makes muscle, Schwann makes Schwann, dermis ranges widest — while cells retain memory of where they sat on the limb's axes (Kragl et al., Nature 2009) [established]. Regeneration is nerve-dependent by a quantified threshold: neuregulin-1 from neurons, acting on ErbB2, meets the full three-part test in axolotl — present and lost on denervation, sufficient by bead implantation to rescue denervated limbs to digits, and necessary, since blocking it abolishes blastema formation and substitutes fibrosis (Farkas et al., Development 2016) [established]. Mammals keep a miniature of this strategy: the digit tip regenerates through a blastema-like, nerve-dependent process. The capacity is not absent in mammals; it is fenced to the fingertip.

Sponges — the limiting case. Dissociate a sponge completely and its cells reaggregate and rebuild a functional animal — but only the totipotent archaeocyte fraction can carry reconstitution to completion [established]. The substrate of whole-body rebuilding, at its simplest, is a totipotent cell population inside an architecture simple enough to re-specify.

Why mammals scar instead is multi-factorial, with the weighting honestly unresolved [established factors, speculative weighting]: fibrotic closure is fast and infection-proof, and pre-empts any blastema programme; adult mammalian fibroblasts include scar-lineage states whose blockade permits appendage-bearing regenerative healing in mouse wounds (Engrailed-1 lineage; Mascharak et al., Science 2021) [early-stage, mouse]; the early macrophage programme differs (§2.2); most wounds lack an AEC-equivalent signalling centre and its nerve supply; and mammalian progenitors are fate-restricted with no pluripotent reserve behind them. No single regeneration gene divides the regenerators from the rest. The difference is systems state — which is precisely why it is addressable in principle and unaddressed in practice.

4. Pattern Held Outside the Genome

4.1 The claim, precisely

Every cell maintains a resting membrane potential, Vmem, set by its ion channels and pumps; cells couple through gap junctions into electrical networks that propagate voltage states and small signalling molecules across whole tissues. The Levin programme's strong claim: the spatial pattern of Vmem across a tissue is an instructive prepattern — a code specifying which organ forms, where, how many, and with what polarity — such that anatomy can be edited by editing voltage, without editing DNA. The components grade separately, and this section grades them.

4.2 What is demonstrated

In Xenopus embryos, voltage-reporter dyes reveal a stereotyped hyperpolarisation pattern across the nascent face — an “electric face” — before any craniofacial structure exists; perturbing it deforms the face, and restoring it rescues development against continued teratogen exposure [established observation; rescue early-stage]. Cells of the presumptive eye field take a characteristic Vmem signature and express Pax6; blocking the voltage change abolishes eye gene expression in roughly half of embryos (Pai et al., Development 2012) [established in Xenopus]. Misexpressing ion channels that converge on that same voltage range in gut, back, or tail — territories where Pax6 alone cannot induce an eye — produced complete eyes, lens, retina, and optic nerve included, at ectopic sites [established in Xenopus]; tadpoles relying on tail eyes alone passed a light-cued learning assay [early-stage, small cohorts].

The planarian experiments are the programme's centrepiece. Intact Dugesia japonica exposed for three days to the gap-junction blocker 1-octanol, washed out, then amputated: 25 percent regenerated two heads, 72 percent normal, the rest abnormal (Durant et al., Biophys J 2017) [established phenomenon, Levin laboratory]. Two-headed worms re-amputated months later, never re-treated, regenerate two-headed in perpetuity — and the normal-looking majority are cryptic carriers, again yielding roughly 25 percent two-headed progeny on re-amputation, all with wild-type genomes. A transient bioelectric intervention (an H⁺/K⁺-ATPase blocker) resets the population permanently back to one head. In a related species, transient treatment produced head shapes stochastically matching other planarian species from a single genome — though there the rewrite remodelled back over weeks, an internal limit on permanence often left unquoted [established phenomenon, same lineage].

In cancer models, tumour-like structures induced by human oncogenes in frog embryos are depolarised before any histological abnormality, and forced hyperpolarisation — including expression of the hyperpolarising channel at a distance from the oncogene-expressing cells — significantly reduced tumour incidence despite continued oncogene expression; the suppression chain runs through the butyrate transporter SLC5A8 to HDAC inhibition, and depleting butyrate-producing microbes abolished the distant effect [established in Xenopus; no human oncology result]. The strongest translational result in this intellectual lineage is not purely electrical at all: adult frogs — a non-regenerative stage — given a twenty-four-hour wearable bioreactor delivering a five-drug cocktail regrew paddle-like, innervated, sensing limbs over eighteen months (Murugan et al., Sci Adv 2022) [early-stage].

4.3 The critics, on the record

The ledger must be carried whole. The high-profile results — permanent two-headed conversion, cross-species head shapes, ectopic complete eyes, distant tumour suppression — originate overwhelmingly from one laboratory and its collaborators, in two model organisms, at stochastic penetrance (25 percent, not 100), with morphology scoring that has not been subjected to independent, blinded, pre-registered replication [established as the state of the literature]. Voltage dyes report relative fluorescence, not calibrated millivolts, so precise “voltage codes” are overstated. The workhorse drugs are dirty: octanol perturbs membranes and many channels, so attributing its effects specifically to gap-junction information outruns the controls — and the molecular route of §3.2 produces the identical two-headed phenotype with clean genetics, so parsimony does not yet favour a separate voltage store until the two are causally ordered, which no experiment has done. No bioelectric intervention has produced a therapeutic result in a mammal. The “software,” “pattern memory,” and “cognitive” vocabulary is a heuristic layered on real physiology — generative as metaphor, [speculative] as explanation.

Graded verdict. Vmem is a real, manipulable, instructive variable in embryonic and regenerative patterning in frog and planaria, capable of triggering whole organ programmes and of durably altering regenerative targets [established in those models]. A general bioelectric code, readable and writable across species, and voltage as the locus of anatomical memory: not demonstrated. For the synthesis (§10), voltage enters as a candidate for the pattern layer — with the muscle positional-gene map of §3.2 standing beside it as the molecular store any voltage signal would have to write into.

5. The Borrowed Builders — Viral Genes as Architects of New Body Structures

5.1 The accounting

About eight percent of the human genome is recognisable endogenous retrovirus — and roughly half is transposable-element legacy of one kind or another [established]. Of the retroviral loci, 85–90 percent are solitary LTRs left when recombination deleted the genes between them; nearly all are mutated and epigenetically silenced, and no replication-competent human endogenous retrovirus is known. The host spends serious machinery — KRAB zinc-finger proteins evolved in an arms race with successive viral waves — keeping them off, and derepressed retroelements are pathogenic in ageing and disease models [established]. Domestication is the exception. But the exceptions are load-bearing, and two of the body's most boundary-obsessed systems — the placenta and the immune system, the organs whose whole problem is negotiating self and other across an interface — are disproportionately built from viral parts [established as enrichment; any larger reading is speculation].

5.2 Syncytin: the stolen key that built the vessel

A retroviral envelope protein does one thing supremely well: it fuses a membrane to a membrane. Captured in the germline, that tool fuses cells to cells — and can therefore build a syncytium, a tissue with no internal borders, which is what the placenta's working surface is.

Syncytin-1, the envelope of HERV-W, was captured in the primate lineage roughly 25–30 million years ago; syncytin-2, from HERV-FRD, more than 40 million years ago. In both, the virus's other genes decayed while env alone was kept under purifying selection — the signature of domestication [established]. The mechanism is the virus's own, step for step: the precursor is cleaved into surface and transmembrane subunits; the surface subunit binds its receptor (for syncytin-1, the amino-acid transporter SLC1A5; for syncytin-2, the lipid transporter MFSD2A); binding drives the transmembrane subunit to fold its heptad repeats into a six-helix bundle that winches the two membranes into a fusion pore. In the placenta, villous cytotrophoblasts fuse continuously into the overlying syncytiotrophoblast — one continuous multinucleate layer, some ten to thirteen square metres at term, bathed directly in maternal blood, performing exchange, hormone synthesis, and barrier duty. Without continuous fusion the layer cannot grow or be repaired. Knockdown of syncytin in human trophoblasts reduces fusion and hormone secretion in vitro [established]; the causal proof belongs to the mouse:

Mice independently captured their own pair, syncytin-A and syncytin-B, one for each of their placenta's two syncytial layers. Syncytin-A knockout embryos die at E11.5–E13.5: the first syncytiotrophoblast layer fails to form, unfused cytotrophoblasts pile up, fetal vessels fail to invade the labyrinth, and the embryo dies of placental failure while otherwise initially normal (Dupressoir, Heidmann et al., PNAS 2009) [established]. Syncytin-B knockout is viable with milder defects; the double knockout dies earlier. After capture, development was rewired around the viral gene until it became a single point of failure.

The catalogue does not stop at two. Systematic genome screens find at least ten independent captures, each from a different retrovirus into a different lineage — squirrel, rabbit, carnivore, hyena, ruminant, tenrec, opossum, and, outside Mammalia altogether, a viviparous lizard [established as a catalogue; necessity proven only in mouse]. The pattern tracks placental architecture: hyenas, which uniquely shifted to blood-bathed placentation among carnivores, carry their own lineage-specific capture, while their pangolin cousins, with non-invasive placentas, carry none — a phylogenetic control written by evolution itself. The standing hypothesis — that each capture handed a lineage a new fusogen with new invasiveness, around which a new maternal–fetal interface was built — fits the distribution [early-stage as theory].

And the placenta's viral inheritance negotiates as well as builds. Syncytin-2 retains the retroviral immunosuppressive domain, functional in standard assays, positioned exactly at the interface where a half-foreign fetus must not be refused; a second captured envelope, suppressyn, regulates the first by blocking its receptor [established for the assays; the tolerance role early-stage — maternal–fetal tolerance is multiply redundant, and no in-vivo proof isolates this contribution]. The vessel and its diplomatic immunity arrived in the same stolen package.

5.3 Gag captures: the vessel's maintenance crew and the brain's courier

Peg10, derived from a sushi-ichi retrotransposon's Gag-Pol, is essential twice over. Its knockout dies around E10.5 of placental failure (Ono et al., Nat Genet 2006) [established]; mutating only its viral protease motif spares early development but kills perinatally through defects in the fetal capillaries of the placenta — an early construction function and a later vessel-maintenance function, separable in one gene. RTL1, an independent Gag capture from the same retrotransposon family, likewise maintains the fetal endothelium at the interface, its deletion and its over-dosage both lethal around birth [established]. Two viral captures, cooperating obligately in one structure. Peg10's Gag also forms virus-like particles that package its own mRNA — now engineered as an endogenous delivery vehicle (the SEND lineage) [early-stage biotechnology].

Arc completes the argument. This immediate-early gene, required for long-term memory consolidation, encodes a homologue of retroviral Gag — domesticated independently in tetrapods and in flies. Arc protein self-assembles into virus-like capsids, encapsulates its own mRNA, is released from neurons in extracellular vesicles, is taken up by neighbouring neurons, and is translated there in an activity-dependent manner (Pastuzyn et al., Cell 2018) [established for capsid formation and transfer in culture and mouse]. Whether blocking the transfer alone, sparing Arc's synaptic functions, would erase a specific memory in a behaving mammal has not been shown [gap]. Even so, the exhibit stands: the brain's plasticity machinery runs, daily, on a working virus part operating by virus logic. “Viral” and “self” are historical categories, not functional ones.

5.4 The interferon network is wired with viral promoters

Sixty million years before syncytin's diplomatic career, a gammaretrovirus-like family, MER41, scattered more than a thousand LTR copies through an anthropoid ancestor's genome — each LTR carrying binding sites for STAT1 and IRF1, the transcription factors of the interferon-γ response. Lying near immune genes, the copies were co-opted as a ready-made inducible enhancer network. The decisive test: deleting the single MER41 LTR upstream of AIM2 — the cytosolic DNA sensor that assembles an inflammasome — abolished AIM2's induction by interferon entirely (Chuong, Elde & Feschotte, Science 2016) [established by CRISPR deletion]. A viral promoter is a necessary component of a human innate-immune sensor's regulation. Parallel co-options wire early-embryo and placental gene expression across dozens of LTR families [established in the studied cases].

Read §5 whole: evolution's own transition technology was capture and governance — foreign builders admitted, silenced where dangerous, and set to work exactly at the body's boundaries of becoming. No engineer in §10's synthesis proposes anything evolution has not already run.

6. Distributed Systems Without a Controller

6.1 Physarum: optimisation and learning in a single cell

The plasmodial slime mould Physarum polycephalum is one syncytial cell with millions of nuclei, extending tubes whose diameters adapt to flow: high-flux tubes thicken, low-flux tubes retract. In a maze, the plasmodium first fills the corridors, then retracts the dead ends, leaving a tube along the shortest path between food sources in the majority of trials (Nakagaki et al., Nature 2000) [established behaviour]. Given oat flakes at the positions of Tokyo and thirty-five surrounding cities, with light standing in for costly terrain, its pruned network matched the real rail network on the experimenters' chosen metrics — transport efficiency and fault tolerance comparable, at slightly lower total length, by about twenty-six hours (Tero et al., Science 2010) [established as performed]. A short flow-reinforcement equation reproduces such networks in simulation, which cuts both ways: the achievement is real, and it is the achievement of a local rule, not of a designer.

The learning claim is stronger and survives its controls. Plasmodia made to cross repellent-but-harmless quinine or caffeine bridges slowed less and less over five to six days until they crossed as controls do; the aversion recovered spontaneously after two days' rest, and was stimulus-specific — quinine-habituated animals still avoided caffeine — meeting the standard behavioural criteria for habituation while excluding fatigue and sensory adaptation (Boisseau, Vogel & Dussutour 2016) [established for this paradigm]. Then the result this thesis cannot stop looking at: habituated plasmodia allowed to fuse with naive ones transferred the habituation — one trained partner sufficed, in series of more than two thousand animals per condition — but only when fusion had lasted three hours, by which time a vein had formed at the junction; pairs separated at one hour showed no transfer [early-stage, single group]. Something material crosses the new vessel and carries the acquired state. That something has never been identified [gap — the central gap of this literature]. The mould also reads its own extracellular slime trail as an external “already searched” mark, and will override the mark for a rich reward — memory without learning, deposited regardless of experience [established behaviour]. What Physarum offers the synthesis is not a component — its transferred factor cannot be bottled until it is named — but a proof: distributed control, state transfer through living junctions, and adaptation without any controller exist in a cell with no neurons at all.

6.2 Fungal spiking: recordings established, language not

Differential electrodes in mycelium-colonised substrate record extracellular spike trains in four fungal species, clustering into trains whose parameters vary by species and which increase when hyphae meet new food (Adamatzky, R Soc Open Sci 2022) [established as recordings]. Segmenting the trains statistically yields a “vocabulary” of up to about fifty train-types with a length distribution superficially resembling English word lengths. The claim to language is [speculative], and its own author files the null hypothesis himself: propagating hyphal tips are electrically charged, so a passing tip registers a spike whether or not anything is being said. No spike train has been shown to cause a specific response in a recipient hypha, to be decoded by the fungus, or to carry referential content; no playback experiment exists. Underneath the overclaim sits a defensible physiology — fungi do run action-potential-like events and calcium waves coupled to growth, damage, and nutrient contact [early-stage]. At most, on current evidence, spikes could report local state variables across centimetres. Nothing combinatorial has been demonstrated.

6.3 The wood wide web: an audit

Common mycorrhizal networks exist — one fungal genet can colonise several plants' roots — and isotope tracers move between plants through fungal pathways in laboratory microcosms [established]. The forest-scale edifice built on that base — ubiquitous networks, mother trees provisioning kin seedlings, canopy-wide warning signals — does not survive audit. Karst, Jones & Hoeksema (Nat Ecol Evol 2023) counted the mapped-field evidence: five studies that genotyped fungi to map networks in the field, covering two forest types, two tree species, three fungal species; across field studies, network-mediated transfer increasing seedling survival or growth was inconsistent or unsupported; tracer movement had been repeatedly cited as tracer benefit, with the fungus's own retention as a sink rarely excluded; and positive results were cited disproportionately while caveats evaporated from review to review — a documented distortion cascade [established as an evidence audit]. What survives: mycorrhizal symbiosis is essential to most land plants, and hyphal networks redistribute nutrients within the fungus's own economy. The cooperative forest superorganism is metaphor. It is recorded here at length because the author's vision traffics in living networks — and her standard, applied throughout this thesis, is that a network claim is worth exactly its mapped evidence, no more.

7. Substrates That Can Be Built

7.1 The leaf that held a heartbeat

Perfuse a spinach leaf's own veins with detergents — in the tabulated protocol, ten percent SDS for five days, then Triton in bleach — and what remains is a translucent cellulose ghost of the leaf with its hierarchical vasculature intact, from midvein to veinlet (Gershlak et al., Biomaterials 2017) [established as proof of concept]. Particles the size of red blood cells traverse the network. Seed the lumen with human endothelial cells and they line the vessels; seed the surface with human stem-cell-derived cardiomyocytes and the cells adhere, handle calcium, and beat — for up to twenty-one days in culture. Decellularised apple does the analogous trick in three dimensions: mammalian cells invade its hundred-to-three-hundred-micrometre pores, proliferate for weeks, and implanted under mouse skin it vascularises from the host with no overt rejection at study endpoints (Modulevsky, Pelling lineage) [established in vitro and rodent]. No leaf has ever been perfused with blood in a living body, integrated electrically with a heart, or stacked into thick tissue that survived. The method generalises across leaves and roots; the function does not yet.

7.2 The material that is alive

Grow a skin of Ganoderma lucidum mycelium in liquid culture under conditions that preserve its thick-walled resting cells — the chlamydospores that commercial processing kills — and the material can do what no dead scaffold can: puncture or tear it, return it to humidity, and within about two days surviving chlamydospores revive, throw new hyphae across the breach, and functionally heal it, with partial mechanical recovery (Elsacker et al., Adv Funct Mater 2023) [early-stage, first characterisation]. The same living material survives dry, nutrient-free storage and revives — dormancy as a materials property; printed mycelium lattices likewise self-repair and regenerate after damage (Nature Materials 2023) [early-stage]. The limits are written into the achievement: the skins are fragile, orders of magnitude weaker than leather or engineered tissue, and a living material is incompatible with sterilisation, implantation, and dry clinical storage. Its path, if any, runs outside the body first.

7.3 The two hard limits

Resorbability. Humans have no cellulase. Native plant cellulose therefore does not resorb on clinical timescales — the apple scaffold persisted essentially intact in mice while being politely ignored, the whole problem in one result. Oxidise the cellulose and it hydrolyses but loses its architecture; accept permanence and accept the long foreign-body ledger; or switch to mammalian matrix, which the immune system can eat in sequence (§2.3) — currently the only substrate family with human implant experience. Any claim of a biodegradable plant-organ scaffold without a degradation dataset is [speculative].

Vascularisation. Viable tissue lives within roughly 100–200 micrometres of perfusion. A leaf solves this beautifully in two dimensions and fails in three: stacked leaves do not anastomose into a blood-tight hierarchy, endothelial lining is patchy at branch points, and plant vessels are uncharacterised under arterial pressure and whole blood. This single limit defeated thick engineered myocardium, defeated VX-264's starved islets (§2.4), and bounds every substrate in this section. Perfusion, not scaffold chemistry, is the rate-limiting layer of the entire field — and the rate-limiting fact of this thesis.

8. The Signal Molecules

8.1 Psilocybin and psilocin: a window-opener, measured honestly

Pharmacology. Oral psilocybin is a prodrug, dephosphorylated to psilocin, which agonises cortical 5-HT2A receptors — Gq signalling through phospholipase C to calcium and PKC, increased glutamate release, BDNF, TrkB, mTORC1, synaptic protein synthesis [established]. A second, location-based mechanism is demonstrated in vitro: cortical neurons hold a large intracellular 5-HT2A pool that serotonin itself cannot reach without its transporter, while membrane-permeant psychedelics can; impermeant analogues bound the surface receptor yet produced no dendritic growth until let inside (Vargas et al., Science 2023) [established in vitro and mouse; human significance early-stage]. Direct binding of psilocin and LSD to TrkB itself, at sub-nanomolar affinity in one laboratory's assays, with a knock-in mutation (Y433F) that abolishes plasticity while sparing the head-twitch response (Moliner et al., Nat Neurosci 2023), is graded [early-stage, contested]: the mutation also impairs BDNF signalling itself, and no independent group has reproduced the binding at comparable affinity. That TrkB signalling is required downstream of psychedelic plasticity is [established]; that the drug binds the receptor directly remains unproven.

Growth. A single psilocybin dose in mice raised dendritic spine density and spine-head width by about ten percent within twenty-four hours in medial frontal cortex, driven by an elevated formation rate, with a fraction of new spines persisting at least a month and excitatory transmission increased (Shao et al., Neuron 2021) [established in mouse]. No human structural imaging at spine resolution exists.

The human inflammation study, in full. Bradley, Kelley et al. (JAMA Psychiatry, published online 21 October 2026; NCT04932434): a nonrandomised, single-arm, open-label pilot — twelve participants aged 40–75 with mild-to-moderate Parkinson's disease and mood dysfunction, oral synthetic psilocybin 10 mg, then 25 mg two weeks apart, plasma biomarkers in nine of the twelve at baseline, one day after each dose, and thirty days after the second. The proinflammatory index (mean z-score of IL-6, IL-8, TNF-α, IFN-γ, hs-CRP) fell by standardised mean differences of −0.72 (p=.003), −0.60 (p=.02), and −0.62 (p=.047) at the three post-dose points; individual markers reached significance only one day after the first dose; IL-10 rose transiently; the mitochondrial-stress marker GDF-15 fell after each dose but not at thirty days, while FGF-21 trended the other way [early-stage]. The authors' own limits govern the reading: no control group, no blinding, nine plasma samples, false positives possible despite correction, and both mitokines sensitive but not specific. A composite that persists while its components do not is exactly the silhouette of a multiple-comparison artefact; a randomised replication with pre-registered endpoints is the deciding experiment, unrun. Preclinical work supplies a plausible mechanism — 5-HT2A agonism potently suppresses TNF-α-driven inflammatory signalling in peripheral tissues in animals (Nichols lineage) [established preclinically] — but no psychedelic study in any species has measured antigen-specific tolerance, regulatory T-cell induction, macrophage switching in a wound or graft, graft survival, or an autoimmune outcome [established absence]. In gatekeeper language: a systemic set-point nudged toward resolution-phase biology, in nine patients' blood. Promising as immunology of a disease state. As immunology of transition: untested.

Delivery. Intravenous psilocin exists to make the window governable: onset in minutes, a roughly two-hour course, and an infusion that can be stopped — reversibility no oral dose offers [established pharmacologically]. TRP-8803 (Entropy Neurodynamics) has completed Phase 1b dosing (n=11) and a six-patient first cohort in binge-eating disorder at Swinburne, reporting improvement in all six — binge episodes down 73 percent at twelve weeks, half the cohort episode-free across the full follow-up — in company and university releases only, with no peer-reviewed publication and no published pharmacokinetic table [early-stage, company-reported]. The peer-reviewed IV literature is older and smaller: 2 mg intravenous psilocybin in fifteen experienced volunteers measurably reorganised cerebral blood flow and network coupling (Carhart-Harris et al., PNAS 2012) [established]. The gaps are itemised in §11; the largest is that nothing anywhere in this literature touches §2's gate directly.

8.2 Cordycepin: a real molecule inside a borrowed legend

Taxonomy first, because the literature's commonest error is conflation: the ant-manipulating Ophiocordyceps, the caterpillar fungus O. sinensis (whose commercial Cs-4 is a fermented mycelium of a different isolate), and cultivated Cordyceps militaris, the actual source of cordycepin, are three different subjects [established].

The manipulator works from the body inward. At the biting moment, fungal cells form an interconnected three-dimensional network through the host's muscle — up to a quarter of mandibular muscle volume in reconstructions, atrophying the fibres that lock the death grip — while never invading the brain, which stays structurally intact and functionally necessary, since a dead brain cannot climb (Fredericksen et al., PNAS 2017) [established]. Dual RNA sequencing at the bite finds the fungus enriched for secreted proteins, proteases, and an unusually large family of bacterial-like enterotoxins, while host immune and chemosensory genes are turned down — the host's refusal machinery quieted until positioning is complete [early-stage, correlative; no single effector has been knocked out and shown necessary]. Manipulated brains, uninvaded, still show altered neuromodulators and markedly elevated ergothioneine, a fungal antioxidant — the fungus plausibly preserves the organ it steers [early-stage interpretation]. No Ophiocordyceps infection of any mammal is documented; host specificity and mammalian body temperature bar the fictional version outright [established absence].

Cordycepin itself — 3′-deoxyadenosine, an adenosine missing the oxygen that lets a chain continue — is honest biochemistry [established in vitro]: imported by nucleoside transporters, phosphorylated to an AMP analogue that activates AMPK's energy-stress pathway, and onward to a triphosphate that terminates RNA synthesis and shortens poly(A) tails, falling hardest on short-lived inflammatory transcripts. Its fatal flaw is one enzyme: adenosine deaminase destroys it in blood within minutes. The fungus co-produces pentostatin, an ADA inhibitor, to protect it; their joint clinical trial in leukaemia enrolled fourteen patients and produced no established efficacy; the engineered prodrug NUC-7738, which slips past all three resistance steps, shows up to fortyfold greater potency in assay and early Phase 1/2 signals without randomised data [early-stage]. Human trials of Cordyceps preparations for immune outcomes are small and low-grade — modest NK-cell activity changes in short studies, the largest rigorous trial (n=332, kidney disease) without a published result in open sources. Verdict: a genuine molecule with genuine mechanisms, and no established human immunological efficacy [established absence]. The legend of the puppet fungus is true — for ants. Its chemistry is a pharmacology lead, not a transition technology.

9. The Plant Instruments — Existence Proofs

The author's plant set enters the evidentiary record here, each member read for the programme it actually runs. None rebuilds a body. Each demonstrates, in working form, a design principle the synthesis needs.

9.1 The flytrap counts

Each trigger-hair touch fires an action potential and a cytosolic calcium wave. Two stimulations within roughly thirty seconds — the decay constant of the first calcium transient is the memory — sum past threshold and snap the trap in about a tenth of a second by a snap-buckling instability (Suda et al., Nat Plants 2020) [established]. The count continues: action potentials three through five, generated by a struggling captive over hours, switch on the jasmonate pathway in proportion to their number — hermetic sealing, acid and hydrolase secretion, and induction of nutrient transporters including a high-capacity sodium channel, DmHKT1, whose expression scales with the count and which keeps toxic sodium cargo electrically separate from the excitable tissue doing the counting (Böhm et al., Curr Biol 2016) [established]. A mutant, DYSC, fires normal potentials and normal calcium signals yet neither snaps nor digests; jasmonate rescues its digestion but not its closure — the lesion sits in decoding, between signal and reading — and the decoder gene remains uncloned [early-stage; gap]. Two principles generalise: analogue integration with a decaying memory window is a real counting mechanism built from one ion; and the same jasmonate cassette that makes an ordinary leaf defend itself makes this leaf digest — carnivory by co-option of defence into digestion, the plant mirror of §5's viral captures.

9.2 The pitcher keeps a pool and a populace

A pitcher is a pitfall: a wettable rim insects aquaplane off, a fluid that acidifies from near-neutral to pH 2–3 on opening and capture, and a proteome — nepenthesin I and II, aspartic proteases stable to about sixty degrees with an acid optimum no mammalian enzyme tolerates, alongside chitinases, glucanases, peroxidases, most of them pathogenesis-related proteins — defence, again, re-pointed at digestion [established]. Feeding the plant prey or chitin induces jasmonate and the digestive programme, mirroring the flytrap on a days-long clock [established]. But the pool is not sterile: it is a managed micro-ecosystem of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, mosquito larvae, and, in one species, a mutualist ant that dives the fluid and fishes prey out — correlated with the plant dialling its own digestion down where the ant works — while another species rents its pitchers to roosting bats and collects a third of its leaf nitrogen from their faeces [established; the partitioning of digestion between plant enzyme and microbe unresolved — gap]. Applications today are a niche laboratory reagent, a preclinical coeliac-disease result in mice, and polyester hydrolysis at bench scale [early-stage]. The principle: digestion as an ecology under governance, not a single fluid — a substrate that hosts a working community and tunes it.

9.3 The corpse flower spends heat as signal

The titan arum's spadix does not merely warm; it heats in pulses, waves rising base to tip past 36 °C against a 27 °C understory, synchronised with waves of odour, so the column of hot air works as a chimney lifting the scent plume above still air (Barthlott et al., Plant Biol 2009) [established]. The furnace is mitochondrial respiration deliberately short-circuited — alternative oxidase shunting electrons past the proton-pumping complexes so their energy lands as heat — fuelled by massive starch mobilisation (Zulfiqar et al., PNAS Nexus 2024) [established]. The scent is methionine dismantled into dimethyl disulfide and trisulfide, with putrescine newly identified beside them: rotting-flesh mimicry precise enough, with the meat-red spathe and the warmth itself, to hire carrion insects as pollinators. The upstream trigger of the burn remains unknown [gap]. The principle: energy spent not on structure but on the fidelity of a signal — heat as punctuation, timed in pulses, because a transition announced badly does not happen at all.

10. The Combination — What a Living Substrate Would Have to Be

Read across all ten strands, a substrate capable of carrying mammalian tissue into a new form needs five layers at once, because every failure in this record is one layer missing:

  1. An identity layer. The new tissue must be accepted as self — by hypoimmune editing (HLA silencing, CD47 raised, autoimmune targets accounted) or by re-educating the host (transient chimerism, regulatory T-cell maintenance). Status: partial human form — one edited graft surviving drug-free; tolerance protocols real for dozens of kidney patients (§2.3–2.4).
  2. A timing layer. A scaffold whose degradation rate is tuned to macrophage recruitment, releasing the signals that walk the inflammatory-to-remodelling trajectory across its one-to-two-week clock. Status: established preclinically; the design rule exists and is ignored by most substrate research (§2.3).
  3. A perfusion layer. Pre-formed, endothelialised, anastomosable vasculature at no more than 200-micrometre spacing through thick tissue. Status: does not exist. This is the report's central negative finding (§7.3).
  4. A pattern layer. Instructive cues that tell fate-restricted progenitors which form to build — today, morphogen and growth-factor cocktails and the bioreactor precedent; voltage codes are candidates whose causal order against the molecular map is unsettled. Status: in humans, healing to the local default only (§§3–4).
  5. A plasticity layer. A transient window — a hormone pulse in the insect pattern, a psychoplastogen-class loosening in neural tissue, a partial-reprogramming cycle in the mammalian attempt — during which structure loosens for remodelling while retained infrastructure is protected. Status: demonstrated in animals; human use preclinical (§§3.1, 8.1).

Combinations needing fewer layers already work, and the correlation is exact: hypoimmune islets (identity + replacement, no pattern change), ECM-remodelled muscle (timing + scaffold, default pattern), zimislecel (replacement + rented permission). Local transitions are established or early-stage medicine precisely because they are partial. The whole-body version — a substrate that carries a person through a transitional phase into a new body vessel form — awaits layer three and a mammalian layer four, and is graded [speculative] without apology.

What remains metaphor, for the record: the forest that thinks, warns, and mothers through fungal networks (§6.3); fungi speaking a fifty-word language (§6.2); a caterpillar dissolving into soup and being reborn (§3.1 — the reality, retained infrastructure under selective demolition, is the more useful model); cordyceps piloting a mammal (§8.2); immune suppression as the key to regeneration — directly tested and insufficient (§2.2); and any psychedelic induction of tolerance (§8.1 — never measured). Against those, the deepest non-metaphor in this thesis runs the other way: the placenta, the interferon network, and the memory protein Arc are, by knockout and deletion proof, built from captured viruses. A body already is an assembly of governed transitions. The open question is only whether the layers can be operated deliberately, in an adult, on a clinical timescale. Layer by layer: partially. All at once: not yet.

The vision, returned to its author with its verdict. The formation she named — trap, pool, furnace-flower, and the fungus that re-instruments its host — does not carry a body into a new form, and no honest reading can make it do so. What the formation proves, member by member, is the grammar of such a carrying: count before you close (the flytrap), digest by governed ecology (the pitcher), spend energy on the signal's fidelity (the corpse flower), re-instrument from the body inward while preserving the organ you need (the cordyceps), and admit foreign builders under strict governance at the boundary (the retroviruses). The carrier itself was found where the title of this thesis said it would be: in the immunology — the substrate of life that conducts every transitional phase of becoming the record contains.

11. The Open Register — Gaps Carried, Not Filled

  1. TRP-8803 / IV psilocin pharmacokinetics: no peer-reviewed Cmax/Tmax/AUC table exists in the open literature; all figures beyond design targets are company or exchange-filing statements.
  2. JAMA Psychiatry 2026 numeric table: standardised effects were published in prose; absolute cytokine concentrations were not extractable, and the result awaits randomised replication with component-level endpoints.
  3. TrkB direct binding by psychedelics: single-group, unreplicated at comparable affinity; the knock-in control is not binding-specific.
  4. The flytrap's calcium decoder (DYSC): phenotype established, gene uncloned.
  5. The Physarum transfer factor: habituation crosses a fusion junction in a material form; the material is unidentified.
  6. The corpse flower's thermogenic trigger: mechanism downstream is transcriptomically mapped; the upstream signal is unknown.
  7. Bioelectric causal order: whether voltage state is upstream of, downstream of, or parallel to the Wnt/positional-gene system is unshown; independent blinded replication of the high-profile results is sparse.
  8. Carlsson hypoimmune islets: n=1; dose fraction reported as ~5–7 percent across sources; durability beyond fourteen months, autoimmune recurrence rate at curative dose, and long-term CD47 safety are unknowable yet. The Harlan attribution critique is unanswered in print.
  9. Pitcher digestion partitioning: plant enzyme versus microbial contribution is unmeasured.
  10. Psilocybin and the gate: no study, in any species, has tested a serotonergic agent against graft survival, macrophage programming in a wound, or autoimmune relapse. The experiment is obvious and unrun.
  11. Mammalian whole-body transition: blocked at the perfusion layer (§7.3) before all other questions become practical.
