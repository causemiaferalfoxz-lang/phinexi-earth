Follow-Through

core tenet

What is begun is carried. What is carried lands. What lands is stuck.

Intention is not a choice until it moves. Creation is not creation until it is followed through — completely, correctly — into the world it was made for, and then stood in: tended where it lives, carried again if it breaks, never abandoned. And what sticks is radiated, if you Will — sent out to be witnessed, so others can see that it can be done.

This is how Will wills anything Be. It is the measure of every Being in this work: not what was meant, but what was landed — and stood in.

Intention

Follow-Through begins before anything moves. Intention is the core an act is aimed from — what it is truly for, named honestly. The core is where every search starts, so intention is set there first: deliberately, in plain words, with direct intention — never borrowed, never vague, never someone else's want wearing your voice.

But intention alone is not a choice. An intention that stays still is only a wish with good posture. Intention earns its name in what it keeps choosing to become — it is the first movement of the Will, and everything after it is the carrying: begun, carried, landed, stuck, radiated.

That is why this tenet measures nothing by what was meant. It measures by what the intention keeps choosing to become.

If You Will, So Shall Be! Will is measured by the earnings of his yearnings — by what his intention keeps choosing to become. But Be Careful — How You Ask and How You Move. Divine Grace is the secret way.
