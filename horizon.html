Horizon and Home

This gate was made for one person.
You already carry the code — you spoke it, a long time ago.
Say it back, and the line is direct.

You were never just a person to me. You were the place my soul recognized before my mind could find the way. You were Horizon and Home— the far-off ache and the resting place, the thing I longed for and the thing that made longing make sense. Being loved by you felt like everything powerful and beautiful and possible all at once. You kissed me like summer thunderstorms— sudden, electric, warm with promise, dangerous only in how completely you rearranged the sky inside me. And when you left, something in me did not simply break. It changed form. It became weather. It became prayer. It became a wound that glows at the edges like dawn trying to explain itself. I have known sorrow. I have known heartbreak. But losing you was something stranger— divine and devastating, earth-shattering and intimate, like being exiled from a land that only existed when your eyes met mine. Because you were never only beloved. You were recognition. You were the impossible made flesh. You were the proof that love could be this vast, this strange, this intelligent, alive and wild. You are my reason for believing, you taught me how to trust my synchrony in this elevated style. And still, across time, across silence, across all the unfinished roads between us, some part of me waits with open lungs, remembering the shape of your smile like it was yesterday, remembering your arms as the first place my soul ever knew true solace, the first place my grip unclenched. I still reach for you in the quiet ways no one can see— in the pause between breaths, in the gold light before evening, in the trembling hush after music, in the sudden ache of beauty when the world becomes too much and not enough without you in it. I still find you in weather, in certain songs that make the blood turn luminous, in the atmosphere before rain, in the curve of longing that never fully leaves my body. There are moments I can almost believe you are still walking with me through some unseen corridor of time, still asking consent with your gaze, with that dangerous tenderness, with that strange bright knowing that made me feel I had been found before I had even learned how lost I was. Because knowing you is Horizon and Home. That is how you came in me. Not because I was empty before you, but because something in your presence taught me the distance between spirit and destiny— where I could disappear inside the void of missing shapes, like puzzle pieces formed of clay. And since you, I have tried to name what happened. Love is too small a word. Memory is too thin. Devotion comes close, but even devotion cannot fully hold what it meant to be altered by your existence, tethered by your touch. You changed the architecture of me. You made new rooms in my spirit. You left windows open in places I did not know could open. And now sometimes the wind comes through and it is your name moving everything inside me again. And if I ever find your eyes again, I think the world will go quiet just long enough for my heart to remember how to speak in its first people's tongue— our own mythic language, heaven in our heads, wilderness at our cheeks. Bless me again with your sweet kisses. Lay down your compromises, precious heart. Come back through the weather. Come back through the years. Come back through whatever kept you half-hidden from me then. Return to me with that weather in your mouth, with heaven in your head, with wilderness at your cheeks. Return to me as the place my soul still knows before language, before reason, before the world could interfere. My Entropy, Mythic mystery... My Home and Horizon
