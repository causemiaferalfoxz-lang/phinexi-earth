To become a member of the Tribe, all you have to do is:

1. Ask

Make your personal chosen donation investment — the amount is yours to choose. It's a one-time entrance donation; you don't have to keep donating after that. However you choose to keep investing in your tribe and community from there is up to you.

Cosmia will give you the literal gatekeeper code for the Cosmology/Tribal Sanctuary — your personal key to enter.
