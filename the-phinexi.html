A core tenet

Follow-Through

What is begun is carried.

What is begun is carried. What is carried lands. What lands is stuck. Intention is not a choice until it moves. Creation is not creation until it is followed through — completely, correctly — into the world it was made for, and then stood in: tended where it lives, carried again if it breaks, never abandoned. And what sticks is radiated, if you Will — sent out to be witnessed, so others can see that it can be done.

  1. Begunwhat is begun is carried
  2. Carriedwhat is carried lands
  3. Landedwhat lands is stuck
  4. Stuckstood in — tended where it lives, carried again if it breaks, never abandoned
  5. Radiatedsent out to be witnessed, so others can see that it can be done

This is how Will wills anything Be. It is the measure of every Being in this work: not what was meant, but what was landed — and stood in.

This tenet is spoken to the creators — all of them. Whoever begins a world, a work, a venture, a life inside this cosmology is measured the same way: not by the beauty of the beginning, but by whether it was carried all the way into the world it was made for, and stood in once it landed. Without Follow-Through, Will is purposeless. Yearningless. You need Follow-Through to truly and properly care.

Intention

Follow-Through begins before anything moves. Intention is the core an act is aimed from — what it is truly for, named honestly. The core is where every search starts, so intention is set there first: deliberately, in plain words, with direct intention — never borrowed, never vague, never someone else’s want wearing your voice.

But intention alone is not a choice. An intention that stays still is only a wish with good posture. Intention earns its name in what it keeps choosing to become — it is the first movement of the Will, and everything after it is the carrying: begun, carried, landed, stuck, radiated.

That is why this tenet measures nothing by what was meant. It measures by what the intention keeps choosing to become.

Will is measured by the earnings of his yearnings — by what his intention keeps choosing to become.

But Be Careful — How You Ask and How You Move. Divine Grace is the secret way.

Grace is not seized and it is not forced. It is soft, and it is Source — there if you will it, in how you ask and in how you move. Ask by invitation, never imposition. Move completely, and correctly.
