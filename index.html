01 / Orientation

Begin simple.

The whole world can be entered in five movements.
Nothing else has to be understood first.

01

Arrive.

“Tell the truth about the room.” Before meaning, notice conditions: body, place, people, land, hunger, noise, pain, money, time, fear, desire. Do not call yourself a failure when the actual truth is that the room is on fire.

02

Choose.

“Name what you are here for.” Witness, clarity, action, ritual, rest, translation, or simply company: choose the job before opening more of yourself. A practice without intention gets hijacked by habit.

03

Consent.

“Draw the Doorway before you cross it.” Decide what may be touched, remembered, shared, or transformed. Decide what stays closed. Consent is not a one-time entrance fee; it stays alive throughout the practice.

04

Create.

“Let the truth take a body.” Make a sentence, movement, boundary, meal, sigil, map, repair, piece of code, or practical act. Creation is where Purpose stops being decoration and starts having consequence.

05

Return.

“Choose what happens to the offering.” Keep it, correct it, release it, act on it, or leave it unfinished. Then record enough context that your future self can meet the moment without being trapped inside it.
